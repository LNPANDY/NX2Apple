**v1.1.1: A new engine with Mario Kart World and more, better game covers, the iPhone shell in six languages, an iPhone Duo side column, and fixes for SideStore, file names and Mac app builds.**

## English

**Update note.** Games you built before this update are rebuilt on the first Play, because the shader chain of the engine moved again. This is the normal rebuild path and needs no action.

**Fixed**
- **Wrong game covers**: the library picks the base game's cover and not a Switch 2 Edition, a bundle or a demo. Of the 167 titles with several shop products, the new rule picks the game for 164. A cover an older launcher saved is checked once and replaced. Three titles stay unsure (Cadence of Hyrule, Dragon Marked for Death, Police Simulator). A banner with a promotion painted into it, such as "Demo Available", cannot be told apart from the data. Manage and Change artwork sets another one. Reported by @zatteogit (#403, #632)
- **iPhone shell texts in your language**: about 120 texts were English in German, Swedish, French and Italian, and three error messages were English in every language. All are in six languages now. The new texts are machine translated and wait for review (#631)
- **Touch layout editor**: it has two clear resets, one for the chosen control and one for all controls that asks first. It scrolls on a small phone, and Done is translated (#623)
- **SideStore install of a shared IPA**: a game with a very large shader tree made the IPA fail to install, because a ZIP holds at most 65,535 entries. The shaders now ship packed in one file and the shell unpacks them on Play. Reported in #587, by @Silverhza (#593)
- **iPhone Duo rotation**: the shell turns with the device again and has the tabletop layout. Phones play games in landscape only. By @jonochocki (#642)
- **Games with an upper case extension**: a game file called `GAME.NSP` or `.XCI` is found and built (#554, #650, #653)
- **Mac app on a disk without an atomic swap**: building the app on such a volume, an exFAT drive for example, now replaces the old app by moves and no longer fails. Before it starts, the build checks the output disk's format and says what to do (#595, #651, #654)
- **Switch 2 games in the Mac app's settings**: the game language row shows for a game that has a NUP update (#619, #647)
- **Games from a dump folder or a NUP without control data**: the Game language picker shows on the Mac and on iPhone and iPad, read from the game's RomFS. By @LNPANDY (#668, #671)
- **iPhone and iPad shell**: the quit prompt says that the app closes and you open it again (#620, #648). The game data stays out of the iCloud backup (#618, #649)
- **Windows**: the engine recovery no longer crashes, and the cached SDK's broken `.tbd` stubs are repaired before every probe (#613)

**New**
- **iPhone Duo side column**: the Controllers and Settings chips and Back sit in a narrow column next to the screen, placed by the areas the system reserves. Phones without such an area look as before. Checked in the Simulator only (#622, #633)
- **Preparing game**: after Play, the shell shows "Preparing game…" while it unpacks the shaders and hands over to the engine, and ignores the controller until then. By @Silverhza (#652)
- **Engine update**: the pin moves to `777a5f6c618a` with 105 upstream commits. By upstream's notes the Mac gets Mario Kart World and Mario Tennis Fever (Switch 2) at 60 fps, Super Mario 3D All-Stars as one game of several programs, Mario & Luigi: Brothership, DAVE THE DIVER and Kirby Star Allies 4.0.0a. Breath of the Wild and Animal Crossing: New Horizons have a picture for a taller screen such as the iPhone Duo's. Fixes for single games include Pokémon Violet 3.0.1, Sparks of Hope, Smash Ultimate single player, Alien: Isolation and Burnout Paradise. Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom no longer drops to 30 fps, because Mac apps switch Game Mode off. Fn and F switch a Mac game to full screen. Shader builds take about half the CPU. A failed pipeline archive is only a warning. A NUP without a base game packs. We did not play any of it (#689)
- **Picture shape**: a new setting for games that have it, such as Breath of the Wild. The game draws a picture in the shape of a taller screen, for example the iPhone Duo's. The texts are machine translated (#689)

**Known issues**
- **Switch 2 covers**: of the five Switch 2 games only Donkey Kong Bananza has a store cover. The other four show the engine's screenshot or the placeholder (#632)
- **iPhone Duo side column is not run on a Duo**: only in the Simulator. A native navigation bar instead of our own column is a follow-up (#622)
- **Machine-translated texts wait for review**: the new German, Swedish, French and Italian texts of the iPhone shell, the new failure row of the Mac app builds, and the Picture shape texts (#631, #651, #689)
- **The new engine has not run on a device**: no game was played with it on a Mac, an iPhone or an iPad. Super Mario 3D All-Stars was built for the Mac host only (#689)
- **Mario Tennis Aces after the update**: our own fix for its crash is gone, because upstream fixed the same crash another way for Mario Tennis Fever. We did not check Aces with it (#689)

**Requires**
- iOS or iPadOS 26 or later
- To build NVN games on a Mac: Xcode 27.2 and macOS 26.6 or later

## 中文

**更新提示。** 更新前构建的游戏会在第一次点 Play 时重新构建，因为引擎的着色器链又变了。这是正常的重建流程，无需任何操作。

**修复**
- **游戏封面不对**：游戏库现在选基础游戏的封面，而不是 Switch 2 版、套装或试玩版。在有多个商店商品的 167 个游戏里，新规则为 164 个选对了游戏本身。旧版启动器保存的封面会检查一次并替换。有三个游戏仍不确定（Cadence of Hyrule、Dragon Marked for Death、Police Simulator）。图片里画着「Demo Available」之类宣传的横幅无法从数据里区分，可用「管理」里的「更换封面」换一张。由 @zatteogit 报告（#403、#632）
- **iPhone 壳 App 的文字用你的语言**：约 120 条文字在德语、瑞典语、法语和意大利语里是英文，另有三条错误信息在所有语言里都是英文。现在六种语言都有了。新增文字为机器翻译，等待审校（#631）
- **触控布局编辑器**：有两个清楚的重置，一个重置所选按键，一个重置全部按键并会先询问。小屏手机上可以滚动，「完成」已翻译（#623）
- **用 SideStore 安装共用 IPA**：着色器文件很多的游戏会让 IPA 安装失败，因为一个 ZIP 最多只能有 65,535 个条目。现在着色器打包成一个文件，壳 App 在点 Play 时解包。在 #587 里报告，作者 @Silverhza（#593）
- **iPhone Duo 旋转**：壳 App 又会随设备旋转，并有桌面（半开）布局。手机上的游戏只能横屏。作者 @jonochocki（#642）
- **扩展名为大写的游戏**：名为 `GAME.NSP` 或 `.XCI` 的游戏文件现在能被找到并构建（#554、#650、#653）
- **在不支持原子替换的磁盘上构建 Mac App**：在这类磁盘（例如 exFAT 驱动器）上构建 App 时，现在改用移动方式替换旧 App，不再失败。构建开始前会检查输出磁盘的格式并说明该怎么做（#595、#651、#654）
- **Mac App 设置里的 Switch 2 游戏**：有 NUP 更新的游戏会显示游戏语言一行（#619、#647）
- **来自转储文件夹或没有控制数据的 NUP 的游戏**：Mac、iPhone 和 iPad 上现在会显示游戏语言选项，语言从游戏的 RomFS 读取。作者 @LNPANDY（#668、#671）
- **iPhone 和 iPad 壳 App**：退出提示说明 App 会关闭，由你再次打开（#620、#648）。游戏数据不再进入 iCloud 备份（#618、#649）
- **Windows**：引擎恢复不再崩溃，缓存 SDK 里损坏的 `.tbd` 桩文件会在每次探测前修复（#613）

**新增**
- **iPhone Duo 侧边栏**：「控制器」和「设置」按钮以及返回键放在屏幕旁的窄列里，位置由系统保留的区域决定。没有这种区域的手机外观不变。只在模拟器里检查过（#622、#633）
- **正在准备游戏**：点 Play 后，壳 App 在解包着色器并交给引擎期间显示「Preparing game…」，并在此期间忽略手柄。作者 @Silverhza（#652）
- **引擎更新**：引擎固定点移到 `777a5f6c618a`，包含 105 个上游提交。出自上游的说明，Mac 上新增 Mario Kart World 和 Mario Tennis Fever（Switch 2），均为 60 fps，Super Mario 3D All-Stars 作为一个含多个程序的游戏，以及 Mario & Luigi: Brothership、DAVE THE DIVER 和 Kirby Star Allies 4.0.0a。Breath of the Wild 和 Animal Crossing: New Horizons 有适合更高屏幕（例如 iPhone Duo）的画面。单个游戏的修复包括 Pokémon Violet 3.0.1、Sparks of Hope、Smash Ultimate 单人模式、Alien: Isolation 和 Burnout Paradise。Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom 不再掉到 30 fps，因为 Mac App 会关闭游戏模式。Fn 加 F 让 Mac 游戏进入全屏。着色器构建大约只占用一半的 CPU。管线存档失败时只显示警告。没有基础游戏的 NUP 也能打包。我们没有玩过这些内容（#689）
- **画面形状**：适用游戏（例如 Breath of the Wild）的新设置，游戏会绘制适合更高屏幕（例如 iPhone Duo）的画面。文字为机器翻译（#689）

**已知问题**
- **Switch 2 封面**：五个 Switch 2 游戏里只有 Donkey Kong Bananza 有商店封面。其余四个显示引擎截图或占位图（#632）
- **iPhone Duo 侧边栏没有在真机 Duo 上运行过**：只在模拟器里检查过。用系统原生导航栏代替我们自己的列是后续工作（#622）
- **机器翻译的文字等待审校**：iPhone 壳 App 新增的德语、瑞典语、法语和意大利语文字，Mac App 构建的新失败提示，以及「画面形状」的文字（#631、#651、#689）
- **新引擎没有在设备上运行过**：没有人用它在 Mac、iPhone 或 iPad 上玩过游戏。Super Mario 3D All-Stars 只为 Mac 主机构建过（#689）
- **更新后的 Mario Tennis Aces**：我们自己针对它崩溃的修复已移除，因为上游用另一种方式修复了 Mario Tennis Fever 的同一个崩溃。我们没有用它检查过 Aces（#689）

**系统要求**
- iOS 或 iPadOS 26 或更高版本
- 在 Mac 上构建 NVN 游戏：Xcode 27.2 和 macOS 26.6 或更高版本
