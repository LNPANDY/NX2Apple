**v1.1.1: Better game covers, the iPhone shell in six languages, and a tidier touch layout editor.**

## English

**Fixed**
- **Wrong game covers**: the library picks the base game's cover and not a Switch 2 Edition, a bundle or a demo. Of the 167 titles with several shop products, the new rule picks the game for 164. A cover an older launcher saved is checked once and replaced. Three titles stay unsure (Cadence of Hyrule, Dragon Marked for Death, Police Simulator). A banner with a promotion painted into it, such as "Demo Available", cannot be told apart from the data. Manage and Change artwork sets another one. Reported by @zatteogit (#403, #632)
- **iPhone shell texts in your language**: about 120 texts were English in German, Swedish, French and Italian, and three error messages were English in every language. All are in six languages now. The new texts are machine translated and wait for review (#631)
- **Touch layout editor**: it has two clear resets, one for the chosen control and one for all controls that asks first. It scrolls on a small phone, and Done is translated (#623)

**Known issues**
- **Switch 2 covers**: of the five Switch 2 games only Donkey Kong Bananza has a store cover. The other four show the engine's screenshot or the placeholder (#632)
- **iPhone Duo side column**: the launcher still leaves the strip next to the screen empty (#622)
- **Machine-translated texts wait for review**: the new German, Swedish, French and Italian texts of the iPhone shell (#631)

**Requires**
- iOS or iPadOS 26 or later
- To build NVN games on a Mac: Xcode 27.2 and macOS 26.6 or later

## 中文

**修复**
- **游戏封面不对**：游戏库现在选基础游戏的封面，而不是 Switch 2 版、套装或试玩版。在有多个商店商品的 167 个游戏里，新规则为 164 个选对了游戏本身。旧版启动器保存的封面会检查一次并替换。有三个游戏仍不确定（Cadence of Hyrule、Dragon Marked for Death、Police Simulator）。图片里画着「Demo Available」之类宣传的横幅无法从数据里区分，可用「管理」里的「更换封面」换一张。由 @zatteogit 报告（#403、#632）
- **iPhone 壳 App 的文字用你的语言**：约 120 条文字在德语、瑞典语、法语和意大利语里是英文，另有三条错误信息在所有语言里都是英文。现在六种语言都有了。新增文字为机器翻译，等待审校（#631）
- **触控布局编辑器**：有两个清楚的重置，一个重置所选按键，一个重置全部按键并会先询问。小屏手机上可以滚动，「完成」已翻译（#623）

**已知问题**
- **Switch 2 封面**：五个 Switch 2 游戏里只有 Donkey Kong Bananza 有商店封面。其余四个显示引擎截图或占位图（#632）
- **iPhone Duo 侧边栏**：启动器仍然让屏幕旁边的条带空着（#622）
- **机器翻译的文字等待审校**：iPhone 壳 App 新增的德语、瑞典语、法语和意大利语文字（#631）

**系统要求**
- iOS 或 iPadOS 26 或更高版本
- 在 Mac 上构建 NVN 游戏：Xcode 27.2 和 macOS 26.6 或更高版本
